Clean accuracy vs robustness tradeoff scatter
    - x = clean acc
    - y = AUC_robust or ASR@ε
    - color = type/family
    - This answers: “Are quantum/hybrid robust because they’re weaker, or robust despite being weaker?”

Efficiency vs robustness scatter
    - x = training time (or inference cost)
    - y = robustness summary
    - This answers: “Is robustness gained at unrealistic compute cost?”

Stability vs robustness
    - x = across-seed std of clean acc (or robustness)
    - y = robustness
    - This answers: “Are the robust models consistent or fragile?”
