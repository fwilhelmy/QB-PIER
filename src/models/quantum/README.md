# Quantum Models

Quantum models rely on PennyLane to build variational quantum circuits and layers.

- `VQC.py` – single variational quantum circuit module.
- `ParallelVQC.py` – execute several circuits in parallel.
- `Quanv2d.py` – quantum convolutional layer.
- `ansatz/` – parameterised circuit templates used by the models.
- `embedding/` – classical-to-quantum feature encoding strategies.

These components enable building fully quantum networks or mixing them with classical layers.
